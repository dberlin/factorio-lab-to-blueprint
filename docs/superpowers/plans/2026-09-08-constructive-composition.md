## 2026-09-09: collaborative design revision — bounded complete-path templates

**Status: unimplemented recommendation, not a new factory result.** The design
integrator and graph reviewer exchanged proposals and counterexamples, rather
than producing independent reviews. This section supersedes the *next-step
recommendation* above, not its retained results, commands, fixtures or hashes.

### What the evidence does and does not establish

`Flight.points()` fixes each global departure two cells outward from its
terminal, each arrival two cells outward, and an X-middle horizontal path.
`finish()` then chooses heights for those immutable XY paths. The retained
mall contradiction at `(604,83)` and `(967,83)` therefore disproves that path
choice, not the existence of a route or factory. An acyclic precedence graph
would still be insufficient: its longest chain or same-plane conflicts can
exceed the catalog's finite legal heights.
Main also replayed both retained mall paths over **all 576 pairs of legal
global heights 3–26**: every pair has a full-segment intersection. The executed,
source-hashed result is
`docs/superpowers/evidence/2026-09-06-feasibility-first/capacity-first-design-review/fixed-cycle-height-replay.json`.
This establishes failure of the old fixed XY pair, not success of any
proposed selector.

There is also a different, height-independent access obstruction. Main's
independent expansion of the **planned** ground approaches in retained
`capacity-first-cases/*/flights.json` found intersecting approach pairs:
control **0**, mall **12**, 0.75× **6**, 1.25× **12**, universe **0**.
Mall flights 42/163 meet at `(361,51,0)`; flights 43/162 meet at
`(361,52,0)` and `(361,53,0)`. These are queued global approaches, not the
already-emitted partial links audited historically. No duplicate global
riser XY columns were found. Thus distinct riser columns, cycle removal and
a clean partial-link audit together still do not establish simultaneous
endpoint access.
The calculation and source hashes are retained separately in
`docs/superpowers/evidence/2026-09-06-feasibility-first/capacity-first-design-review/retained-geometry-analysis.json`;
the historical experiment evidence was not rewritten.

The rate qualification is equally important. In the current constructor,
`connect()` records `rate` in `ConstructedLink`; it does not program a belt
rate. `splitter()` constructs a junction without a rate schedule, and the
tree's exact continuation sums are accounting, not a demonstrated allocation
policy. The reviewer traced the existing lane-balance check to a capacity
relaxation and the rate propagation to fair-share estimates, not guaranteed
service bounds. Passing all 67 registered checks therefore remains valuable
validator/codec evidence but is **not proof of simultaneous rated operation**.
In particular, the historical control's 8,213-building, 67-check result is
unchanged; no new in-game or dynamic-rate result is claimed here.

### Alternatives considered together

1. **Selected: a bounded, incomplete selector over complete 3D templates.**
   Each candidate chooses terminal adapters, both risers, horizontal shape
   and legal height together. Select one compatible candidate per positive
   obligation, considering fixed local interfaces and all other candidates.
   No height-order theorem is needed: full-segment conflict checking is the
   safety criterion. This can remove a cycle by moving a column or horizontal
   segment, and can change an overlapping ground approach. It may still
   refuse a solvable factory; completeness and mall success are not promised.
2. **Alternative decomposition: an exterior two-plane switchyard.** Re-place
   the same strips in banks with private escape gutters; assign unique
   endpoint X columns and connection Y rows. Carry X segments on one legal
   plane, Y segments on another, with designated interchange columns.
   Its proposed height use is constant instead of growing with graph depth,
   at the cost of long paths and area. However, escaping every real strip and
   junction into those columns, keeping interchange risers out of other
   paths, and making all perimeter terminals accessible still need a
   construction proof. Merely drawing an X/Y grid does not provide that
   proof. Do not implement this as a claimed universal fallback.
3. **Rejected as the principal architecture: SCC-guided detours.** Rebuild
   fixed-path precedence, choose the lexicographically first cycle edge,
   try at most four outward one-dogleg detours of that flight, accept the
   first full-segment-safe change that strictly decreases the number of
   cyclic dependency edges, and stop after at most the initial number of
   such edges or the first non-improving step. This is finite and useful as
   a diagnostic. The descent rule is not complete, can discard a necessary
   temporarily worse move, and does not cure ground-access cuts or finite
   height exhaustion. It cannot be the original-mall acceptance argument.

The selection is a **compiled corridor-template experiment**, not a rename
of unrestricted routing: it never searches arbitrary grid neighbours,
invents obstacle-following waypoints, widens placement after failure, or
calls the global router. If this narrow family fails, report that result.

### Conceptual contracts and ownership

These are proposed immutable records, not APIs already implemented:

| Record | Required content and owner |
|---|---|
| `FrozenFactory` | Original canonical spec and SHA-256; machine/recipe counts and exact rates; import/export/surplus contract; technology, stacking and deadline. Only the fixture owns these facts. |
| `PhysicalInventory` | Actual machine-buffer, sorter, lane and junction incidence; producer ownership shared across output ports; each physical capacity and item/stack state; every shared external root's identity, item/domain and player-side service contract. Strip emission owns this inventory, not the flow solver. |
| `FlowCandidate` | Eligible arcs, exact rational positive flows, pool/lane/root/tree-continuation/boundary ledger, stable ordering and candidate ID. A transportation solution is a geometry-aware **candidate**, never an irrevocable final topology or proof of service. |
| `InterfacePlan` | Catalog-derived seats, dock identities/orientations, local trees and links, full reserved volumes, and each exposed terminal's allowed adapters. Interface construction owns every split/merge and its actual configuration. |
| `PathCandidate` | Obligation ID, adapter IDs, legal level, all ordered segments, occupied/clearance volumes, endpoint incidence, item and certified load bound. Geometry owns the entire connection, including departure and arrival. |
| `GeometryCertificate` | One selected candidate per obligation; complete fixed and moving geometry; catalog legality and independent full-segment/port audit. It is not a rate certificate. |
| `RatedRealization` | Actual emitted physical graph, justified junction/sorter/buffer service rules and startup/boundary assumptions, and proof or explicitly labelled observation of simultaneous machine and boundary service. Rates written on graph edges alone cannot inhabit this contract. |
| `Outcome` | Highest gate reached, original-spec identity, consumed work/deadline, certificate or concrete refusal witness; partial geometry is never a completed factory. |

Data flow is `FrozenFactory -> PhysicalInventory -> FlowCandidate ->
InterfacePlan -> PathCandidate selection -> GeometryCertificate`, followed
by physical emission, rate assessment, canonical finalization and complete
factory judgment. The run declares `GEOMETRY_ONLY` or `RATED_ACCEPTANCE`
before starting. Unproved service motifs annotate a geometry-only run as
`RATE_REALIZATION_UNPROVED`; they do not prohibit diagnostic geometry or
validator-complete evidence. Only rated acceptance requires `RatedRealization`,
and its final certificate must refer to the emitted graph.
No phase may derive a new spec or boundary from its own output.

### Selected construction and stopping rule

All numbers and orderings below are proposed **pre-registered experiment
rules**, not tuned or benchmarked values. Before freezing the manifest, require
a cost-only admission probe on the retained inventory: report exact Cartesian
domain cardinalities, bitset/cache memory bounds, and the time/predicate count
for the first 1,024 static candidate checks per case, without selecting paths
or inspecting factory outcomes. For example, `H=24` and 141 obligations permit
846,000 parameter combinations, so an eager 250,000-attempt design cannot
even reach search in the maximal family. This motivates compact lazy domains,
not a claim that larger caps guarantee performance. The admission probe must
show bounded storage and startup filtering inside one tenth of each case's
original budget; otherwise the proposed implementation is not ready for the
original-factory experiment. Freeze the final manifest once after this
cost-only gate and before any control/downstream path selection. No
post-failure parameter sweep or raised 30/60-second allowance is permitted.

1. Start the original absolute layout-through-settlement deadline before
   inventory extraction: **30 seconds for the control, 60 seconds for each
   original mall/universe case**, retaining each fixture's original budget.
   Inventory, exact flow, every candidate, geometry, power, slot binding,
   finalization, all checks and codec work share that one deadline.
2. Preserve captured external/export eligibility and all legal same-item
   internal arcs between different strips. Solve at most **three** exact
   transportation candidates, using deterministic arc iteration:
   captured-first then stable endpoint IDs; nearest-bank-first then stable
   IDs; reverse of the first total ordering. Retain distinct positive-flow
   graphs only. The latter orders change a max-flow choice, not the frozen
   demands, eligibility or producer credits. No optimality claim is made.
   Layout bank IDs are provisional geometry information supplied to flow.
3. For each candidate, plan the existing five-strips-per-bank placement and
   degree-dependent local trees using the same fixed spacing formulas.
   Include degree-one launches, imports, export exits and all local transfers.
   Reserve actual seats, port attachments, approaches and full low-level
   segments before selecting global flights. A conflict in this fixed local
   plan refuses this candidate; it is not repaired by invisibly moving a
   machine, changing a rate or discarding a demand.
4. For each exposed terminal, enumerate five adapter shapes at its endpoint
   height: straight outward distances **2, 4, 6**, or outward **2** followed
   by a perpendicular displacement **+2 or -2**. The resulting endpoint is
   the candidate riser location. Reverse the arrival adapter for flow into
   its dock. Validate straight-approach, turn, orientation and catalog rules;
   an invalid adapter is absent, not shortened into a different template.
   Include adapter segments in occupancy and preserve their port ownership.
5. Form the corridor list once from the planned bank/strip access gaps,
   using integer midpoint lines, plus the two exterior lines eight cells
   beyond the fixed geometry bounds on each axis. For an obligation retain
   at most four X and four Y lines, sorted by added Manhattan length and
   then coordinate. Enumerate the two L shapes, X-middle shapes on those
   X lines and Y-middle shapes on those Y lines. Combine each with the
   at-most-five source and sink adapters and every legal global level
   `3 <= z < canvas.levels`. Thus a domain has at most **250H** candidates
   before deduplication, where `H` is the actual number of legal global
   levels; `H` is never increased. Local transfers remain subject to their
   own catalog limits. Normalize zero-length segments, reject self-retrace,
   and reject any full-segment collision with fixed geometry.
   Represent candidates by their bounded orthogonal segments and exact
   clearance intervals, not a copied set of every traversed cell. Compute
   segment/segment and segment/fixed-volume intersections directly; expand
   cells for the independent audit of selected geometry, not every template.
6. Give each Cartesian parameter tuple a compact candidate ID and keep
   per-obligation live-ID bitsets plus a reversible deletion trail, not a
   materialized path per bit. Decode the constant-size segments only when
   visited. Choose the unassigned obligation with the smallest live-domain
   **upper bound**, ties by obligation ID; this is not eager exact MRV.
   A deterministic iterator visits IDs by total length, maximum height,
   adapter IDs and shape/track ID. Lazily reject static-invalid candidates,
   then compare each candidate with every selected path. After assignment,
   forward-check each remaining domain only until finding one compatible
   survivor or exhausting it, caching static validity and encountered
   incompatibilities. Recurse; restore branch-local deletions on backtrack.
   Static-invalid bits remain deleted. An exhausted domain backtracks.
   Exhaustion is certified only when every ID has been considered/eliminated;
   stopping generation/filtering early is never an impossibility result.
   Compatibility includes all horizontal segments, both full risers,
   terminal adapters, local reservations and endpoint incidence. Never
   commit emitted global paths before a complete assignment is selected.
7. Use a single shared work ledger across all three topology candidates:
   at most **100,000 eligible arcs**, **100,000 exact-flow augmentations**,
   **2,000,000 generated path candidates** including rejected/duplicate
   attempts, **5,000,000 audit-expanded segment cells**, **5,000,000
   primitive interval-pair compatibility predicates** (candidate/fixed and
   candidate/candidate), and **100,000 attempted DFS assignments**, including rejected
   assignments. Stop *before* exceeding any limit; do not retain only a
   truncated domain and then call its failure exhaustive. Check the shared
   deadline inside generation, expansion, flow and search loops and around
   downstream stages. Reuse candidate compatibility results rather than
   recomputing them; no random restart, additional portfolio or reset budget.
8. A successful selection is emitted once through guarded constructors.
   Independently reconstruct occupancy and physical capacities from emitted
   buildings/connections, then power, bind, finalize, judge and encode/decode
   against the unchanged original spec. Audit port poses, full segments and
   clearance again from decoded records, not merely graph-ID equality.
   A downstream failure remains that failure; do not silently select a
   different spec or a router fallback.

These caps bound work and memory as well as search depth; they do **not**
predict that Python can perform that work within the unchanged time budget.
If all fully generated domains for a candidate are searched, its refusal is
`TEMPLATE_FAMILY_EXHAUSTED`. Any generation/search cap is `POLICY_BOUND`;
deadline expiry is `DEADLINE`. `CAPACITY_CUT` requires an independently
checkable exact cut over the complete admitted transport graph, not a
template-pruned graph, and proves only that graph's insufficiency.
Other distinct outcomes are `UNSUPPORTED_INTERFACE` (including
sprayed/prelinked cases), `PHYSICAL_ACCESS_CONFLICT`, `NO_LEGAL_TEMPLATE`,
`RATE_REALIZATION_UNPROVED`, `EMISSION_OR_AUDIT_FAILURE`, and
`FACTORY_VALIDATION_FAILURE`. Separate successful labels are
`VALIDATOR_COMPLETE_WITNESS` and `RATED_ORIGINAL_ACCEPTED`. Retain the
first concrete obstruction and phase/work counters. None proves the frozen
factory infeasible. A failed template must not become a globally forbidden
transport arc: only a physical impossibility certificate could justify that.

### Physical rate and boundary obligations

Credit a strip/item producer pool once, but additionally prove its actual
machine-buffer-to-sorter-to-lane incidence and service. A pool capacity cut
can pass while inaccessible lanes, shared buffers or sorter selection make
its chosen lane distribution unrealizable. Include belt/piler/stack and
sorter limits, merge contention, every continuation of every tree, and each
import root's shared capacity. No cloned capacity at a splitter or root.
Construct one joint capacity witness on this physical graph: independent
reachability flows and fair-share estimates may describe different flows.
Every import/export approach must have one simultaneous reserved construction;
separate perimeter flood fills do not establish concurrent boundary access.

The consumer-visible target is simultaneous original machine and boundary
service, not an arbitrary LP's preferred arc proportions. If a proved
physical policy produces different internal shares, issue a new exact
ledger for those shares **before emission** and certify all admissible
resulting lane loads. Do not check geometry capacities against discarded LP
rates. All required internal consumption, production, exports and allowed
surplus must remain exactly the frozen contract; imports may not be
redistributed through newly player-fed roots.
Any changed shares must stay on the same selected physical graph and within
every selected path's certified load envelope. A new positive arc, changed
topology or larger load invalidates the geometry certificate and refuses
this attempt; there is no unbudgeted re-ledger/geometry repair loop.

Only **rated acceptance**, not the geometric diagnostic, requires an
independently justified service theorem for its admitted motifs under actual
game rules. State the intended service envelope: continuous configured power,
each original import available at its specified sustained rate without extra
roots, original exports continuously able to accept items without invented
throttles, and empty buffers unless the original fixture explicitly permits
seed inventory. Internal production must start from that permitted state;
startup deadlock cannot be cured with unrecorded seed items. The target is the
original simultaneous sustained rates after a justified finite startup, not
exact delivery every tick or success under arbitrary external starvation.
These assumptions must be compatible with the frozen fixture, not overwrite it.
The theorem must cover finite buffers, dependencies, nonstarvation and the
noninterference conditions for composition. Challenge unequal split demands,
unequal merge inputs, chained fanouts, shared-machine output ports and open
exports. Work conservation or a feasible steady-state flow is insufficient.
An unproved motif refuses the **rated claim** while geometry-only evidence
remains reportable; a general formal game-verification project is not a
prerequisite to learning whether the templates resolve the geometry.

A finite-state exact-tick model calibrated to real mechanics could provide
an alternative reachable recurrent-class/service certificate. A bounded
simulation or actual simultaneous blueprint run is useful **observational**
evidence, but not a proof for every admissible boundary/service state. State
which kind of evidence exists. No such model or compositional theorem is
claimed implemented by this revision.

The review's adversary is conditional: a 3/s supply feeding an internal 2/s
demand and an always-accepting 1/s export annotation could allocate 1.5/s to
each under an equal-service splitter model, starving the internal consumer.
The arithmetic refutes the claim that conservation alone enforces the
annotations; it is **not a measured DSP behavior**. Main found no source-port
family mixing internal/export arcs in the five retained unsprayed graphs.
It therefore is not an observed defect in those artifacts. Actual policy
still needs establishing, especially across separate ports sharing a machine
pool. Do not repair the adversary by silently throttling an original export,
assuming a player-paced sink, changing a boundary or inventing priority
settings not present in the actual blueprint.

### Full-segment and endpoint safety

Audit the complete planned construction, not only links emitted before a
refusal. Expand every intervening coordinate of every horizontal and vertical
segment, including terminal adapters and local transfers. Check against
machine footprints, junction bodies, port approach volumes and catalog
clearances; rerun against final projected/slot-bound geometry. Integer
centreline nonintersection is a necessary conservative criterion, not by
itself an in-game paste/clearance proof.

Shared coordinates are permitted only for a declared connection between the
specific owning physical port/link pair with compatible direction and item,
or the junction's explicit catalog attachment arrangement. "It is an
endpoint" is not a general exception: unrelated links meeting at endpoints,
same-item accidental mergers, or duplicated claims on one dock must fail.
The certificate must cover the union of local and global geometry and all
boundary approaches; checking disjoint global flights in isolation fails
this contract.
