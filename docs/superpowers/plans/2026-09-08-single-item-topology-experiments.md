# General single-item topology repair experiments

Status: execution authorized and underway in the isolated `topology-experiments` worktree. Hierarchical v5 execution is separate. All production changes require a demonstrated general mechanism, not a universe-specific branch.

## Problem and non-negotiable rules

The mixed-input-lane ban is intentional and stays. `fa5c6be6` made saturated two-face hosts legal by moving the output collector outside the input rows, then limiting the existing ground-drain strip to one machine. On universe this creates15 or12 separate lab strips, magnifying distribution and approach demand. [Current-source diagnosis](../evidence/2026-09-08-universe-refusal-diagnosis/README.md) preserves18 controlled refusals and exact old/current topology; rates and machine counts did not change.

No experiment may:

- Predicate production behavior on recipe/item/URL names, universe-matrix, corpus index, or reported building IDs.
- Restore mixed-item input lanes, weaken a validator, invent a port, ignore a cargo domain, omit a consumer/coproduced output, or count production twice.
- Claim a raised time/expansion/retry limit, longer producer tail, or renamed existing mechanism is the fix.
- Introduce a persistent cache or a blanket bus backend as a hidden prerequisite.
- Treat a locally legal template as proof the entire factory routes, supplies spray/power, or pastes in-game.

Eligibility must be stated using catalog geometry, attachment inventory, exact demand/capacity, source/sink multiplicity, and routing-access evidence. A treatment must transfer to non-universe cases selected by those properties.

## What already exists: do not reinvent it

- `strip_variants.py` searches ordinary cardinal poses and reachable rows, but **flanked families generate no variants** (`generate_strip_families`). Their legacy emitter chooses a lateral output and drains south at ground level. `StripVariant` currently means one uniformly pitched machine row; it cannot represent arbitrary collector polylines or per-machine yaw.
- `freeform.py` already supports source-family branching, destination-family collection, legal splitter frontiers, family-first routing, conditional splitter guards and up to four first-path alternatives looking for future taps. A ten-tile producer lane has previously routed twelve consumers; tile count is not branching capacity.
- Capacity-bounded shared stacked external trunks and a proliferator supply tree already exist. They do not establish a general complete commodity-distribution skeleton.
- Corridor matching already reserves terminal approaches and can return surveyed partial assignments. Hierarchy already keeps those assignments and tops up missing corridors. An empty missing list is not a complete route.

## Shared experiment protocol

### Frozen inputs and controls

Use baseline `bbc8889d`, explicit PLACED, exact rank, default Tesla, portable band, existing technology-conditioned ramp/altitude and sorter/stack policies. Freeze exact BuildSpec, strip/family instances, packed placement, routing bounds, coater nodes, supply/demand and parent budget at the appropriate boundary. Diagnostic snapshots are trusted local artifacts, not a new shipping cache/format.

For each treatment keep:

1. **C0 shipped control**, including every existing lookahead/reservation mechanism.
2. **Sham control** carrying treatment's ordering/instrumentation but no new geometry/topology decision.
3. **Negative control** that demonstrably fails the relevant invariant; never eligible for promotion.
4. Transfer cases selected before measurement from structural predicates, with counts and exact URLs/specs recorded. Synthetic geometry fixtures are labeled synthetic, never passed off as real recipe builds.

Preparatory work, enumeration, assignment and routing all count toward the same production budget. Separately funded exhaustive/local diagnostic sweeps must be labeled as such and cannot establish same-budget production improvement. Keep original failures; serial adjudication is a separate population.

User ruling (2026-09-08, topology only): use exact retained failure replays and the smallest relevant behavioral cases during hypothesis iteration. Run independent experiments in parallel; do not serialize for hypothetical load concerns. Rerun only failed cases serially, retaining the original failures. Do not run full test suites or the72-cell audit merely to determine whether a Universe treatment fixes its failure. The broader promotion gate below applies only after a treatment earns promotion consideration. Hierarchical v5's separately accepted verification plan is unchanged.

### End-to-end acceptance

All applicable existing physical, single-item-flow, exact rate, sorter/belt/piler capacity, spray ancestry, power and projection checks must pass on the **same emitted candidate**. Encode/decode the bytes and retain their hash. Game-paste acceptance remains unmeasured without actual game access.

Primary result: complete valid factory coverage at the unchanged30-second corpus operating point, both freeform and sequence-pair. Report area/belts/splitters and phase work; flag >20% area expansion against paired clean controls and historical dense references. No “improvement” obtained by enormous sparse templates goes unreported.

Promotion requires: all six universe cells in two original rounds CLEAN, no lost CLEAN cell across the72-cell paired corpus, no invalid/crash/grace overrun, and non-universe structural transfer. A failed target remains FAIL even if the mechanism is useful locally. Bounded local failure disproves only that treatment domain, not all legal factories.

**Seams:** `_prepare_routing_problem`, `_PreparedRoutingProblem.new_workspace`, the rejected wired candidate before/after compaction/projection, `validate._unsprayed_belts`, `_sprayed_cargo_reaches_machines`, and the existing lane-balance/capacity checks.

Every preparation instead raised `_Unseatable("the reserved proliferator perimeter trunk is occupied", failure=None)`; the aggregate maps that missing failure record to the spray-check text. **Capture:** exact offending item, machine, pickup belt/sorter, all source injections and directed splitter/piler/sorter hops; shortest dirty-path witness from external root, machine injection or orphan run; intended versus actual coater IN/OUT path; source/NetId/strip ownership; exact rate and capacity findings. For output-products capture the five failed approach demands, every enumerated corridor, simultaneous conflicts, enumeration completeness and held/required counts. **Outcome:** classify source/spray bypass, capacity deficit, joint access conflict, or intentional work exhaustion with a concrete witness. Do not choose one cause when multiple independently occur. This is an evidence prerequisite, not a production behavior change or a fifth retry algorithm.

## G1 — Catalog-derived repeatable collector templates

**Hypothesis:** the legacy lowest-east-slot ground drain is not the only legal collector for a saturated-input host. A different authoritative lateral attachment plus an explicitly reserved ramp/dogleg pocket may permit repeated machines while preserving continuous single-item inputs and the original external port count.

**Control:** shipped singleton geometry. Negative control: bypass only the geometric cap on the unchanged ground drain; retained evidence predicts drain/input crossings. Second sham: enumerate poses/attachment rows but retain straight ground drains and baseline pitch.

**Treatment:** an experiment-local immutable physical template containing exact machine origins/yaws, jointly matched input/output sorter slots, collector belt cells/links with Fraction altitude, reserved bounds and existing logical boundary ports. Do not encode collector choices in unrelated yaw/slot fields. Use current catalog/slots/collider/projection APIs and normal emission primitives.

Bounded candidate domain per host/demand/technology case:

- Four cardinal yaws; either lateral collector side; each genuinely reachable lateral slot.
- Collector exit beyond either end of the input band.
- Extra pocket width0, R or2R, where R is the existing ramp-tiles-per-level rule, not a fitted constant.
- Three path forms: straight ground drain; rise-before-input-band/bridge/descent-after-band; outward dogleg into the reserved pocket before the same bridge.
- Available altitude levels only; deduplicate physical candidates; at most256 emitted candidates, interleaved across pose/side/path categories. Record unvisited combinations.

Match output attachment jointly with input slots; do not freeze the first input matching then falsely declare the lateral face unavailable. Widening pitch alone does not eliminate the existing crossing argument. Cardinal rotation of a symmetric host does not create an extra north/south sorter slot.

**Repeatability proof:** find a legal two-machine template, freeze its cell, instantiate3 and6 without resolving each machine independently; test singleton/tail truncation. One continuous input lane per item and the original external port cardinality must remain. Hiding six independent singleton ports inside one rectangle does not count.

**Transfer:** saturated two-face six-input arc-smelter geometry (synthetic); real advanced-mining-machine production fixture with asymmetric assembler inputs (recover its saved URL/rates); unchanged two-input em-rail-ejector flank whose drain is not outermost; refinery/chemical-plant empty-lateral-face controls that must never invent a slot. Require two structurally distinct non-universe controls.

**Kill:** no legal two-machine witness within declared domain; failure to repeat at3/6; mixed cargo, crossing, illegal ramp/sorter, extra external ports, unmodeled supply/power/projection, or success only with technology the URL lacks. Exhausted bounded search is not a proof no other template exists.

**Promotion seam:** only after proof, add explicit collector geometry to the existing atomic family/variant physical plan and have pack/emitter consume the same plan. Remove the corresponding legacy arithmetic; no parallel special-case emitter.

## G2 — Nonuniform repeated cells with explicit local lanes

**Hypothesis:** if G1 cannot separate collector and inputs in one uniform row, a paired/staggered cell or alternating catalog pose can expose a legal collector corridor without multiplying global input ports.

**Dependency:** use G1's exact failure evidence to choose this larger representation experiment, not an arbitrary second backend. Distribution remains unchanged during this axis.

**Treatment domain:** two-machine cells; one or two rows; relative translation drawn from collider-safe pitches and reachable attachment spans; cardinal yaws; continuous, nonbranching per-item local input paths plus one output collector. Enumerate at most256 distinct fully emitted cells per structural case. Freeze a cell and repeat it twice/three times. Do not increase the domain after seeing results; a new domain is a separately preregistered experiment.

The current uniform-X `StripVariant` cannot express this. Use the same explicit experiment-local physical plan as G1 with per-machine XY/yaw and polyline lanes; do not pretend existing pose search already explores it. If a cell needs a new input branching graph, classify that as a combined G2+D1 treatment, not a geometry-only result.

**Controls/transfer:** G1 best legal template or shipped singleton if none; same synthetic saturated host, real non-universe assembler case and empty-face controls. Preserve all rates, domains, technology, external ports and global pack/router.

**Kill:** requires recipe-specific arrangement; per-machine bespoke search rather than repeatable cell; gains require new uncharged distribution; no legal repeated cell within bound; area/ramp footprint eliminates the claimed density benefit. Report representational cost explicitly before any production cutover.

## D1 — Joint complete-family branch/merge skeleton

**Hypothesis:** unchanged singleton strips can route reliably if a commodity family's complete branching and collection obligations are selected together, rather than committing a first leaf with only one future tap guaranteed.

**Eligibility:** connected prepared single-item/single-domain component with multiple sources or destinations. Choose high-fanout and multi-producer components by measured counts/rate imbalance, never recipe identity. Preserve existing strip geometry, terminal ports, coater nodes and bounds.

**Seams:** `_with_sibling_groups`, `_merge_frontier`, `_route`, `_tap_source`, `_pair_lanes`, `_join_shard_islands`, and exact supply/demand before `_PreparedNet` drops rate detail. Current source production is credited once per strip, not once per alternative lane.

**Treatment:** at most two deterministic in-envelope skeleton candidates per selected component using the existing movement graph and legal branch/merge offers. Each skeleton owns directed shared edges, source identity, terminal assignments, exact physical splitter ports, mutually compatible approach/guard reservations, and Fraction edge loads. Use a capacity-feasible forest when one shared trunk cannot carry aggregate load. Keep coater IN/OUT boundaries intact; no downstream unsprayed source injection is permitted.

Select branch-port and terminal-approach assignments for the **complete** family before routing its leaves. A deficit witness names actual branch-port cut, incompatible guards, missing terminal access, source/demand cut or overloaded segment. A count of nominal tap cells is not a witness. Stake transactionally in a fresh existing workspace; route leaf connectors with current primitives; rollback the whole skeleton on attachment failure. Keep every logical terminal identity even where several nets share physical edges.

**Novelty:** complete-family topology and aggregate capacity/access commitments. Shared source paths, family-first order, generic guards and future-tap lookahead already exist and do not count as this treatment.

**Transfer:** fanout below/above producer length across two host families; balanced and surplus/deficit multi-producer components; stack-one and already-shared stacked external feeds; both external/internal supply of one item; sprayed internal/external/both-fed consumers; one-source/one-sink and deliberately overloaded negative controls.

**Proof:** actual directed source-to-demand paths; source production counted once; every shared segment's aggregate load fits its real tier/stack; separate sorter/piler capacity; supplied coater intercepts every physically possible dirty path. `flow.conservation` alone is insufficient because transit capacities there are intentionally not the physical throughput proof.

**Kill:** non-executable terminal connectors, capacity/source-credit error, unsprayed bypass, rollback leak, no transfer, benefit disappears against sham ordering, or failure simply moves downstream. No new limits or all-pairs wiring.

## P1 — Feed joint approach conflicts back into packing

**Hypothesis:** some current output-products failures are jointly incompatible approaches created by a pack, rather than insufficient independent free-neighbor counts. Packing can avoid them using an actual conflict witness instead of repeatedly presenting equivalent unusable terminal geometry to the router.

**Controls:** identical strips and logical distribution, current packing/access matcher (including existing staged clearance repair), and sham identical candidate order with no learned geometry restriction.

**Treatment:** capture a minimal jointly infeasible set from a **complete** bounded approach enumeration at a fixed candidate. Identify contributing host poses and relative placement/clearance relations. Add a conditional packing disjunction requiring at least one implicated relative relation to change; reuse existing packing variables/pose/clearance representation. The cut excludes the proved local configuration, not an item, strip number, absolute corpus coordinate or whole recipe family.

The current access matcher can cut its corridor assignment. This experiment is specifically about soundly projecting an exhausted joint access conflict into the next packing decision. Do not rebrand existing matcher cuts, minimum-pitch repair, height ordering or extra route retries as new work.

Only an exhaustive local witness may create a hard cut. Partial/timed-out enumeration can prioritize a move within the existing schedule but cannot declare the geometry infeasible. Retain the original search/height/repair bounds; charge witness derivation to the same wall. Compare at most the existing admitted candidate sequence—no extra restarts.

**Transfer:** saturated arrivals and departures, multiple terminals sharing a constrained region, differing host poses, an intentionally infeasible local pocket, and a legal case whose enumeration is cancelled. The cancelled case must not become a permanent no-good.

**Kill:** unsound cut excludes a demonstrably routable witness; only nominal free-side counts predict failure; cut relies on item/recipe/absolute identity; gains require extra attempts; or its cause is an E0 spray-source failure rather than pack access. If every captured candidate has a live corridor assignment but fails later dynamic routing, reject this treatment for that capture.
